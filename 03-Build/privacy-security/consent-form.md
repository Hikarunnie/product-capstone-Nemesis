# Musa Consent Form

**Product:** Musa
**Team:** Nemesis
**Document owner:** Team Nemesis
**Last updated:** 01 June 2026

---

## 1. Purpose of This Consent Form

Musa collects limited user data to provide a handmade marketplace experience for creators and buyers. This consent form explains what users are agreeing to when they register, create a studio, publish a product, interact with products, or participate in research and analytics activities.

The consent flow is designed to be:

* **Freely given:** users can choose optional consent categories separately.
* **Specific:** each consent category has a separate purpose.
* **Informed:** users are told what data is collected and why.
* **Unambiguous:** users actively choose consent through checkboxes or clear actions.
* **Withdrawable:** users can withdraw optional consent later.
* **Documented:** Musa stores a record of consent decisions.

---

## 2. User Groups Covered

This consent flow applies to:

| User group            | Examples                                                                   |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Visitors              | People browsing products or pages                                          |
| Buyers                | Users viewing products, adding favourites/cart items, or placing orders    |
| Creators              | Users creating studios and publishing handmade products                    |
| Research participants | Users participating in interviews, usability testing, or feedback sessions |

## 3. Required Processing Notice

Some data is required for Musa to function. This is not optional consent; it is necessary to provide the service.

| Required data          | Purpose                                         | Reason it is required                 |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Account information    | Register, log in, identify the user             | Needed to provide user account access |
| Authentication data    | Keep users logged in securely                   | Needed for account security           |
| Studio information     | Create and display creator studio pages         | Needed for seller features            |
| Product information    | Display handmade products to buyers             | Needed for marketplace functionality  |
| Cart/order information | Support buyer flow and creator order management | Needed for commerce-related features  |

Users are informed that if they do not provide required account or product data, some Musa features cannot work.

## 4. Separate Consent Categories

Musa separates optional consent into clear categories.

### Consent Category A: Product Analytics

**Purpose:**
To understand how users interact with Musa, such as page views, product views, traffic sources, and general usage trends.

**Data involved:**
Page views, visits, referrers, approximate location from analytics, device/browser information, and time-based traffic data.

**Consent statement shown to user:**
“I agree that Musa may use analytics data to understand product usage and improve the platform.”

**Required?**
No. This is optional where consent is required.

---

### Consent Category B: Research and User Feedback

**Purpose:**
To use interview notes, usability testing notes, survey answers, and feedback to improve Musa.

**Data involved:**
User comments, testing observations, pain points, product feedback, and optional quotes.

**Consent statement shown to user:**
“I agree that Musa may use my feedback, interview answers, or usability testing responses for product research and improvement.”

**Required?**
No. Users may use Musa without joining research.

---

### Consent Category C: Marketing and Updates

**Purpose:**
To send product updates, early access information, launch announcements, or creator onboarding messages.

**Data involved:**
Name, email or contact method, creator category, and interest in Musa.

**Consent statement shown to user:**
“I agree that Musa may contact me about early access, product updates, and creator onboarding.”

**Required?**
No. Users can use Musa without agreeing to marketing updates.

---

### Consent Category D: Public Creator/Product Display

**Purpose:**
To display creator studios and product listings publicly to buyers.

**Data involved:**
Studio name, product name, product description, price, category, product image, and creator-provided studio details.

**Consent statement shown to user:**
“I understand that product listings and studio information I publish on Musa may be visible to other users.”

**Required?**
Yes for creators who choose to publish products publicly, because public display is the core purpose of a marketplace listing.

---

## 5. Example Consent UI Flow

During signup, creator onboarding, or research participation, Musa should present separate checkboxes:

```txt
[ ] I agree to Musa’s Terms and Privacy Notice.
[ ] I agree that Musa may use analytics data to improve the product.
[ ] I agree that Musa may use my feedback for product research.
[ ] I agree to receive early access or product update messages.
[ ] I understand that my published studio and product listings may be visible to users.
```

Optional consent boxes must not be pre-checked.

---

## 6. Withdrawal Mechanism

Users can withdraw optional consent at any time.

### Withdrawal methods

Users may withdraw consent by:

1. Sending a request to the Musa team by email.
2. Requesting removal from the early access or research contact list.
3. Asking the team to delete research notes linked to them where deletion is possible.
4. Asking to remove optional marketing contact permissions.

### Contact method

Consent withdrawal requests should be sent to:

```txt
nemesis_musa@gmail.com
```

### Response time

The Musa team will respond to consent withdrawal requests within **30 days**.

---

## 7. What Happens After Withdrawal

| Consent withdrawn              | Result                                                                   |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Analytics consent              | Future optional analytics tracking is stopped where technically possible |
| Research consent               | Future use of identifiable research responses stops                      |
| Marketing consent              | User is removed from marketing/update contact lists                      |
| Public creator/product display | Creator may unpublish or request removal of their studio/listings        |

Withdrawal does not automatically remove data that Musa must keep for security, legal, or service operation reasons, but the team will minimize retained data where possible.

## 8. Consent Storage Record

Musa keeps a consent record to prove that consent was documented.

| Field                       | Description                                              |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| User ID or participant code | Internal identifier for the user or research participant |
| Consent category            | Analytics, research, marketing, public listing, etc.     |
| Consent status              | Accepted, declined, withdrawn                            |
| Timestamp                   | Date and time of consent decision                        |
| Consent version             | Version of the consent form shown                        |
| Collection method           | Signup form, research form, product onboarding, or email |
| Withdrawal timestamp        | Date and time if consent is withdrawn                    |
| Notes                       | Any special request or limitation from the user          |

## 10. Data Minimization

Musa only collects data that is needed for product functionality, research, analytics, or communication. The team avoids collecting unnecessary sensitive information such as government IDs, precise addresses, or payment card data during the MVP stage.

---

## 11. Special Note on Research Participants

For interviews and usability testing, participants are informed that:

* Participation is voluntary.
* They may skip any question.
* They may stop participating at any time.
* Quotes may be anonymized in documentation.
* Personal names are not required in public reports.
* They may ask the team to remove identifiable feedback where possible.

---

## 12. Consent Review

This consent flow should be reviewed when Musa adds new features, such as:

* Online payments
* Delivery management
* Public reviews
* Messaging
* Seller verification
* Third-party marketing tools

Any new data purpose should be added as a separate consent or privacy notice category before launch.
