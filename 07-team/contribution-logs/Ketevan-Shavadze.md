## What I learned

* The most valuable lesson for me was learning how to turn interview evidence into a narrower product decision. At first, Musa was described as a broad handmade marketplace. After analysing ten interviews, we focused the MVP on one measurable creator journey: creating a studio and publishing a structured product listing.
* I also learned that final integration should not be left until the last days. A product can have strong individual parts but still feel incomplete if the analytics, legal files, architecture, README, strategy documents, and presentation are not reviewed together before submission.
