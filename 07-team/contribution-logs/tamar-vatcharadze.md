## What I learned

- The most valuable thing I learned this semester was how to move from raw interview notes to a focused, evidence-based problem statement. Before this course, I thought user research meant collecting opinions. Learning to find patterns across interviews and connect them to a specific, buildable product decision was a skill I had not developed before.
- I also learned that documentation is not separate from the product — it is part of how the team proves what it built and why. Keeping discovery evidence, strategy reasoning, and product decisions organized in the repository made the final submission much stronger than it would have been otherwise.
