## What I learned

- I learned that a product is not only about building features. The hardest part is choosing the smallest flow that actually solves the validated user pain. For Musa, that meant focusing on the creator’s listing and order flow instead of trying to build every marketplace feature at once.
- I also learned that repository discipline matters a lot in a team project. If files, evidence, analytics, and documentation are not organized, even good work becomes hard to prove during checkpoints.
- Another important lesson was that technical decisions should stay connected to discovery. The product became clearer when we kept returning to the real creator problems: fear of starting, unclear pricing, and messy DM-based orders.
