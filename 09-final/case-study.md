# Musa Product Case Study

**Team:** Nemesis
**Product:** Musa
**Live product:** https://musa-front.pages.dev/
**Case-study date:** 12 June 2026

## Executive Summary

Musa is a mobile-first marketplace and storefront platform designed for aspiring and early-stage handmade creators in Georgia. It helps creators move from scattered social-media posts and direct messages to a simple, structured studio where they can publish products and become discoverable in a public marketplace.

The project began with a broad concern: talented creators were making valuable handmade products but often struggled to begin selling consistently online. Through ten user interviews, we found two connected problems. Aspiring creators were delaying their first sales because starting felt intimidating, while early-stage sellers were trying to manage products and customer requests through Instagram, Facebook, direct messages, and handwritten notes. Seven of the ten interviews validated these pains.

Based on this research, Team Nemesis narrowed the product from a general craft marketplace into a gentler first storefront for handmade creators. We built a functional MVP that allows a creator to create an account, set up a studio, add structured product listings, view their own storefront, and appear in an Explore feed.

By 11 June 2026, Musa had received 337 visits, four registered studios, and eight live product listings. These results are still early, but they show that real users were willing not only to visit the product, but also to complete the core creator journey and publish content.
