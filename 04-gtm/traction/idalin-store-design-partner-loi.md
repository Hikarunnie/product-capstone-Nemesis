# Letter of Intent / Design Partner Agreement — Idalin Store

This Letter of Intent documents early design-partner collaboration between **Musa** and **Idalin Store**.

## Parties

**Product:** Musa  
**Team:** Nemesis  
**Design Partner / Pilot Store:** Idalin Store  
**Date:** 2026-05-26

## Purpose

Idalin Store agrees to collaborate with the Musa team as an early design partner and pilot store. The purpose of this collaboration is to test whether Musa helps handmade and small creator stores create a clear online studio, publish product listings, and manage buyer interest outside of scattered social media messages.

## Background

Musa is a marketplace and creator-store tool for handmade creators who struggle to turn social media interest into organized product listings and orders. Many early creators currently rely on Instagram or Facebook messages, which makes it difficult to clearly show prices, availability, product options, and order status.

Idalin Store is included as an early pilot store to help Musa test the real creator-store workflow, including studio creation, product listing, product display, and studio-specific product filtering.

## Collaboration Scope

Idalin Store agrees to:

1. Test Musa’s studio/store page.
2. Test the product listing flow with real or sample products.
3. Give feedback on whether the store page clearly presents products, prices, and creator identity.
4. Give feedback on whether the product flow is easier than managing products only through social media messages.
5. Allow the Musa team to summarize non-sensitive feedback in course deliverables and traction materials.

## Musa Team Responsibilities

The Musa team agrees to:

1. Provide Idalin Store with access to the MVP or demo environment.
2. Help Idalin Store create or test a studio page.
3. Use feedback for product improvement and academic/course deliverables.
4. Avoid publishing private contact information without permission.
5. Treat this as a non-binding design-partner collaboration, not a paid commercial contract.

## Non-Binding Nature

This Letter of Intent is non-binding. It does not require Idalin Store to pay Musa, and it does not require Musa to provide commercial services. It only confirms that Idalin Store is willing to collaborate as an early design partner / pilot store and provide product feedback.

## Confirmation

By signing or approving this document, Idalin Store confirms willingness to collaborate with Musa as an early design partner and pilot store.
