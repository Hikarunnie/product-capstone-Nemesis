# Letter of Intent / Design Partner Agreement — Studio138

This Letter of Intent documents early design-partner collaboration between **Musa** and **Studio138**.

## Parties

**Product:** Musa  
**Team:** Nemesis  
**Design Partner:** Studio138  
**Date:** 2026-05-25

## Purpose

Studio138 agrees to collaborate with the Musa team as an early design partner. The purpose of this collaboration is to test whether Musa helps handmade creators publish products, organize different handmade categories, and reduce the trust and pricing friction that currently makes selling through Instagram difficult.

## Background

Studio138 is a beginner crochet and multi-craft creator. In discovery, Studio138 described making crochet clothes as well as paintings, jewelry, and plushies. Studio138 also described difficulty presenting different product types clearly on Instagram, uncertainty about pricing, and concerns about appearing trustworthy as a new seller.

These problems match Musa's target customer profile: handmade creators who want to start or improve selling online but struggle with product presentation, trust, pricing, and scattered social media workflows.

## Collaboration Scope

Studio138 agrees to:

1. Test Musa's studio/profile page.
2. Test the product listing flow with at least one real or sample handmade product.
3. Give feedback on whether the listing flow feels easier than posting and managing products only through Instagram.
4. Give feedback on product organization, pricing display, trust, and buyer-facing presentation.
5. Allow the Musa team to summarize anonymized feedback in course deliverables.

## Musa Team Responsibilities

The Musa team agrees to:

1. Provide Studio138 with access to the MVP or demo environment.
2. Help Studio138 create or test a studio page.
3. Use Studio138's feedback only for product development and course deliverables.
4. Avoid publishing private contact information without permission.
5. Treat this as a non-binding design-partner collaboration, not a paid contract.

## Non-Binding Nature

This Letter of Intent is non-binding. It does not require Studio138 to pay Musa or require Musa to provide commercial services. It only confirms that Studio138 is willing to collaborate as an early design partner and provide product feedback.

## Confirmation

By signing or approving this document, Studio138 confirms willingness to collaborate with Musa as an early design partner.
