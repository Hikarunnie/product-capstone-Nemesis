## Traction Interpretation

The strongest traction signal is **Studio138** because the creator matches Musa’s ideal customer profile and agreed to collaborate. Studio138 had real product interest but stopped posting because of pricing uncertainty, trust concerns, and difficulty presenting different handmade categories clearly. This makes Studio138 a strong design partner for testing Musa’s studio page, product listing flow, and creator trust features.

The second practical signal is **Idalin Store**, which is used as an early pilot store for testing the creator-store workflow. Idalin helps validate whether Musa correctly supports studio creation, product listings, product display, and studio-specific product filtering.

The remaining entries come from discovery interviews and show repeated demand patterns across handmade creators: difficulty starting, uncertainty around pricing, lack of confidence, trust concerns, and messy Instagram/Facebook DM order management.
